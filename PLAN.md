# Parlo curriculum plan

The weekly update task reads this file. It follows the roadmap and adjusts it to the learner's
progress. Update the "Progress log" section every week.

## Learner

- Complete beginner (CEFR A1). Adult. Lives in the UK.
- Content must stay simple: short sentences, everyday words, present simple before past simple.
- Every flashcard has an emoji picture. Use pictures a beginner can recognise.
- Example sentences use made-up names (Sam, Anna, Tom). Never use the learner's or King's personal details.

## Weekly pack (keep the same shape every week)

- 3 lessons (6–9 phrases each, one with a dialogue), one short tip each
- 20 flashcards, each with an emoji
- 8 quiz questions, at least 3 of them listening questions ("say" + picture options)
- 8 sentences to read aloud
- 3 practice questions with a model answer and useful phrases

## Roadmap

| Week | Topic | Grammar focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello! Me and my family | to be (I'm, he's), have |
| 2 | Food and shopping | like / don't like, Can I have…? |
| 3 | Numbers, time and days | numbers 1–100, What time is it?, days of the week |
| 4 | My home | There is / there are, in / on / under |
| 5 | My day | present simple routines, he/she + s |
| 6 | Clothes, colours and the weather | adjectives, It's cold / sunny |
| 7 | Getting around town | Where is…?, left / right / straight on, bus and train |
| 8 | Health and the doctor | I have a headache, body words, at the pharmacy |
| 9 | Work and jobs | What do you do?, can / can't |
| 10 | Free time and hobbies | like + -ing, at the weekend |
| 11 | Yesterday | past simple: was / were, went, had |
| 12 | Plans | going to, next week |

## How to adapt

- Words with lapses ≥ 2 or in `spellMiss` are "difficult". Put 3–5 of them back into the new week's
  flashcards with a new id and a new example sentence, and practise them in the quiz.
- If he finished fewer than half of last week's lessons, make the next week lighter: 2 lessons,
  15 flashcards, and revise last week's topic before moving on.
- If quiz scores are 7/8 or better and most cards are learned, keep to the roadmap.
- If he has no microphone (`hasMic: false`), keep speaking sentences short.
- If there is no progress data, follow the roadmap.
